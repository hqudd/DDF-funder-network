About this data

Each dot represents a funder that has backed a program with the Doris Duke Foundation (DDF), or a partner that works on one. Partners, such as fiscal sponsors, organizers, administrators, pilot sites and grant recipients, are not co-funders. Funders are circles, partners are diamonds, and grantees (organizations that received a grant from a program) are triangles; grantees are hidden by default, and the switches at the top left show or hide each type. Node color is the DDF area (Arts & Culture, Nature, Health & Well-being), split when a funder works in more than one. Each grant program is filed under one or more of DDF's Big Ideas, and the chips filter by area or by idea. DDF's idea pages name some of these programs; the other placements are our own reading. One idea, Racial Equity in Clinical Equations, has no co-funded program in this data. Node size is the funder's endowment (assets under management for FY23, or total assets from tax filings, 2023-2024; faint nodes had no figure found). Line width is the combined size of the programs two funders share, so it is not either funder's own contribution, which is mostly undisclosed. Amounts mean different things by program (a pledge, a goal, a first round, a cumulative total); each program's detail card says which. Dotted lines mean no amount was found. Faint lines connect funders that co-fund the same program; they show shared membership, not a direct relationship. Each group clusters together. Drag a node to move it. Years are when a program was announced or reported. The two handles set the first and last year shown; Play moves the end handle forward. Research as of Nov 12, 2026.
