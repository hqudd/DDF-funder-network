You · prompt 2

tell me more about DDF's co-funded grants with Ford

Claude

DDF and Ford have two connected efforts in the performing arts, both in 2026. Neither source I read says how the two foundations split the money.

1. Technology infrastructure grants (announced July 6, 2026)

  • DDF announced five grants "in partnership with the Ford Foundation." Together they total more than $4 million over five years.
  • The announcement does not give an amount for any single grant, and it does not say how DDF and Ford share the cost.
  • The grants support these projects:
  • Junebug Productions: an ethical, culturally grounded AI platform for Black theater administration. The project is titled The Future is Us: AI and Afro-Futurism in Black Theater Management.
  • Kinetic Light: research and development of multisensory access technology that links disability arts with performance.
  • The Healing Project (New York Live Arts): art, technology and advocacy aimed at a world centered on healing.
  • Open Circle Theatre: VitaNova, an interactive hybrid musical made accessible to Disabled theatre artists and audiences.
  • Producer Hub: accessible software for remote, real-time jazz performance and recording, built with Marcus Roberts.
  • SOZO Impact and Relaxed Animals: EARTH.SPEAKS, an extended reality platform for the Osage Nation and other Native communities, led by brooke smiley.

The announcement lists six organizations for "five grants." It does not say whether SOZO Impact and Relaxed Animals share one grant or hold two.

Ashley Ferro-Murray, DDF's arts program director, said the grants help "ensure that artists have access to development runways they need for technology innovations."

2. Artists Make Technology (announced Jan 7, 2026)

  • Ford is one of the funders of this initiative, alongside DDF, Mozilla and Hewlett. MacArthur and Omidyar Network joined later.
  • The goal is $11 million over three years, and $8 million had been raised by Oct 28, 2026.
  • Ford's contribution is not disclosed.
  • DDF's page and the July grants announcement do not link the five infrastructure grants to AMT. DDF describes them as part of the same technology strategy.
  • Ford is also a Humanity AI funder, and it was a donor to the 2020 Artist Relief fund and to the Mosaic Network and Fund. Those are on the main list in the spreadsheet.

To find the split, I could look for Ford's own announcement or its grants database. The July announcement also has a short blurb for each grantee that I can pull in full if you want it.

Sources: DDF announcement, July 6, 2026; BroadwayWorld coverage; American Theatre; Mozilla and DDF press release, Oct 28, 2026.

Filled circles have an endowment figure. Faint, mostly hollow circles are funders where I found no endowment figure.
